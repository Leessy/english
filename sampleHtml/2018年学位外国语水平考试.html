The idea is that someone who is treated at the age of 60 will be biologically reverted to 30. But because the therapies are not permanent fixes. Their cells will end up becoming 60 years old again in another 30 years time.

如果让你帮某人实现一个愿望，你会许什么愿望，你最希望帮助谁？
【写作思路】建议依据写作提示进行展开。例如：可以许愿帮助一名失学儿童（a child who has dropped out of school）；重返校园（return to campus）;因为“每个人都有受教育的权利（Everyone has the right to receive education.）”；孩子是祖国的未来（Children are the future of our motherland.）；知识就是力量“Knowledge is power.”
答案解析

解析：例文： What would I wish something for somebody else If I had a chance to make a wish for someone, I would like to make a wish to help a child who has dropped out of school. There are several reasons. First, I think as a child, he or she must return to campus to get more knowledge and improve himself or herself, so that he or she can lead a better life in the future. Second, education should be equal for everyone, and everybody has the right to receive education, in which all of us should devote our help to helping each child to finish their schooling. Third, children are the future of our motherland. As the future generation of our country, they play an important in the development of our economics and science & technology. Finally, we all know that knowledge is power. Only if the children have the opportunity to come back the school to study and get more knowledge, can they do well in their future job and life, and above all, have a bright and promising future. That is why it is vital for us to help the child to return to campus. In a word, if I had the opportunity to make a wish for someone, I would make the above wish for the child who has dropped out of school, because I believe as long as everyone can help each other, our world will become better and better.
