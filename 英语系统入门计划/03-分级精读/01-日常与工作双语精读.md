# 日常与工作双语精读

> 以下文章为本项目原创，集中复用基础语法和高频词块。先读英文，再展开中文核验。

## 1. A Small Daily System（A2）

### English

Ming wanted to improve his English, but his first plan was too large. He tried to learn fifty new words every day. He also watched long videos that he could hardly understand. After one week, he felt tired and stopped.

Then he made a smaller system. Every morning, he reviewed ten useful words and read one short page. At lunch, he listened to the same one-minute recording. In the evening, he used three new phrases to describe his day. The work took only forty minutes in total.

At first, the system seemed slow. However, Ming could remember more because he met the same language many times. After a month, short messages and simple documents no longer looked like a wall of unknown words. He still had a lot to learn, but he knew what to do next.

### 中文核验

小明想提高英语水平，但他的第一个计划太庞大了。他试图每天学五十个新词，还看了自己几乎听不懂的长视频。一周后，他感到疲惫，便停了下来。

后来，他建立了一套更小的学习系统。每天早晨，他复习十个实用词并读一页短文；午餐时，他听同一段一分钟的录音；晚上，他用三个新词块描述自己的一天。所有学习加起来只需要四十分钟。

起初，这套系统看起来进展很慢。但是，由于小明多次遇到相同的语言内容，他反而记住了更多。一个月后，短消息和简单文档不再像一堵由生词组成的墙。他仍有很多东西要学，但他已经知道下一步该做什么。

**重点词块**：`try to`、`at first`、`the same`、`a lot to learn`、`what to do next`

**复述问题**：Why did the first plan fail? What did Ming change? Why did the smaller system work better?

## 2. Asking for Help（A2）

### English

Lina received a new task at work. She understood the goal, but she did not know where the source files were. In the past, she often waited because she was afraid of asking a “simple” question. This time, she wrote a clear message.

“Hi Sam, I’m working on the user guide. As far as I know, the latest files are not in the shared folder. Could you show me where they are? I need them before three o’clock so that I can finish the first draft today.”

Sam replied five minutes later. The files were in a different folder, and he sent Lina the link. Her question was easy to answer because it explained the task, the missing information, and the deadline. Asking for help did not make her look unprepared. It helped the team avoid a delay.

### 中文核验

丽娜在工作中接到了一项新任务。她明白任务目标，但不知道源文件在哪里。过去，她常常因为害怕问出“太简单”的问题而一直等待。这一次，她写了一条清晰的消息。

“嗨，Sam，我正在编写用户指南。据我所知，最新文件不在共享文件夹里。你能告诉我它们在哪里吗？我需要在三点前拿到文件，以便今天完成初稿。”

Sam 五分钟后回复了。文件在另一个文件夹中，他把链接发给了丽娜。这个问题很容易回答，因为它说明了任务、缺少的信息和截止时间。寻求帮助并没有让她显得准备不足，反而帮助团队避免了延误。

**重点词块**：`work on`、`as far as I know`、`Could you show me ...?`、`so that`、`ask for help`

**迁移任务**：把 user guide、source files、three o'clock 换成你真实工作中的内容。

## 3. A Useful Meeting（A2+）

### English

The weekly meeting used to take an hour, but people often left without knowing what to do. The team decided to change the format. Before the meeting, everyone wrote a short update with three parts: what was finished, what was in progress, and what was blocked.

During the meeting, they did not read every update aloud. They only discussed decisions and problems that needed help. When a topic became too detailed, two people agreed to follow up after the meeting. At the end, the team reviewed each action, its owner, and its due date.

The new meeting lasted twenty-five minutes. More importantly, everyone knew the next step. A shorter meeting was not useful simply because it saved time. It was useful because it produced clear decisions and actions.

### 中文核验

每周例会过去常常开一个小时，但人们离开时往往仍不知道该做什么。团队决定改变会议形式。会前，每个人都写一份简短更新，分为三部分：已经完成的内容、正在进行的内容，以及受到阻碍的内容。

会议期间，大家不再逐条朗读所有更新，而只讨论需要决策或帮助的问题。当某个话题变得过于具体时，两个人会约定会后继续跟进。会议结束时，团队再次确认每项行动、负责人和截止日期。

新会议只持续了二十五分钟。更重要的是，每个人都清楚下一步做什么。短会议并不只是因为节省时间才有用，而是因为它产生了清晰的决定和行动。

**重点词块**：`used to`、`in progress`、`follow up`、`at the end`、`the next step`

## 4. Fixing a Small Mistake（A2+）

### English

Kai sent a report to a client and then noticed that one number was wrong. His first reaction was to hope that nobody would see it. Instead, he checked the original data and found the cause. He had copied a value from the wrong row.

Kai corrected the report and sent a second message: “I found an error in the total on page three. The correct number is 18,420, not 18,240. I have attached an updated report. I’m sorry for the confusion.”

The client thanked him for the quick correction. A small mistake became less serious because Kai explained exactly what was wrong and provided the correct file. Hiding the problem would have created a larger risk. Clear communication was part of the fix.

### 中文核验

凯把报告发给客户后，发现其中一个数字有误。他的第一反应是希望没人注意到。可是，他没有回避，而是核对原始数据并找到了原因：他复制了错误行中的数值。

凯改正报告后又发了一条消息：“我发现第三页的总数有误。正确数字是 18,420，不是 18,240。我已附上更新后的报告。给您造成困扰，我很抱歉。”

客户感谢他迅速改正。由于凯准确说明了错误所在并提供了正确文件，一个小错误没有变成严重问题。隐瞒问题反而会造成更大的风险。清晰沟通本身就是修复的一部分。

**重点词块**：`notice that`、`find the cause`、`instead`、`be wrong`、`part of`

## 5. Learning a New Tool（B1）

### English

When Nora started using a new data tool, she wanted to understand every feature before doing real work. She read several long pages, opened every menu, and wrote down many unfamiliar terms. Two days later, she knew many names but still could not complete a basic task.

Her colleague suggested a different approach. First, Nora chose one small goal: import a file, remove empty rows, and export the result. She followed a short guide and repeated the task with three different files. When an error appeared, she read only the section related to that error.

By the end of the week, Nora had not explored the whole tool, but she could use it independently for a real task. She also understood the documentation more easily because commands and terms now referred to actions she had actually performed. The tool became meaningful through use, not through memorizing every feature in advance.

### 中文核验

诺拉开始使用一款新的数据工具时，想在处理实际工作前先弄懂每项功能。她阅读了几篇很长的说明，打开所有菜单，还记下许多陌生术语。两天后，她知道了很多名称，却仍无法完成一项基本任务。

同事建议她换一种方法。首先，诺拉选定一个小目标：导入文件、删除空行，然后导出结果。她按照一份简短指南操作，并用三个不同文件重复了这项任务。出现错误时，她只阅读与该错误有关的章节。

一周结束时，诺拉虽然没有探索完整个工具，却已经能够独立使用它完成一项真实任务。她也更容易看懂文档了，因为命令和术语现在都对应着她亲手完成过的操作。这个工具是在实际使用中变得有意义的，而不是靠提前记住每项功能。

**重点词块**：`before doing`、`a different approach`、`by the end of`、`be related to`、`in advance`

## 6. A Change of Plan（B1）

### English

Mei and Daniel planned to take an early train to another city. When they arrived at the station, they learned that their train had been canceled because of heavy rain. They could wait four hours for the next direct train, or they could take two local trains and arrive only one hour late.

They looked at the route and considered what could go wrong. The local trains required one change, but there was enough time between them. They chose the second option and sent a message to the friend who was meeting them: “Our direct train was canceled. We are taking a different route and expect to arrive at about two. We’ll let you know if anything changes.”

The trip was less comfortable than planned, but it was not stressful. They had compared the options, communicated the delay, and left some extra time for the connection.

### 中文核验

梅和 Daniel 原计划乘早班火车去另一个城市。到达车站后，他们得知列车因暴雨被取消。他们可以等四小时乘坐下一班直达车，也可以换乘两趟普通列车，只晚一个小时抵达。

他们查看路线并考虑可能出现的问题。普通列车需要换乘一次，但两趟车之间留有足够时间。他们选择第二种方案，并给前来接站的朋友发消息：“我们的直达列车取消了。我们正在改走另一条路线，预计两点左右到达。如果情况有变化，我们会告诉你。”

这趟旅程没有原计划那么舒适，却并不让人焦虑。他们比较了方案、说明了延误，还为换乘预留了一些时间。

**重点词块**：`because of`、`what could go wrong`、`expect to`、`let you know`、`extra time`
