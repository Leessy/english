# 基础语法多场景精读

> 本文把同一套基础语法放进生活、学习、工作、出行和技术场景。每组先遮住“解析”列自己判断，再核验。重点不是背整句，而是看懂结构后替换参数。

## 精读时固定标记

```text
S = 主语：谁/什么
V = 完整动词组：做什么/处于什么状态
O = 宾语：动作作用于什么
C = 表语/补语：说明主语或宾语
A = 时间、地点、方式、原因、条件等附加信息
```

不要把每个词都翻译后再拼起来。先说主干 `S + V (+ O/C)`，再加入 A。

## 1. 代词 + be：身份、状态和位置

| 场景例句 | 主干与语法 | 准确理解 |
|---|---|---|
| `I am new here.` | S=`I`；V=`am`；C=`new`；A=`here` | 我刚来这里。不是“我是新的这里”。 |
| `She is my English teacher.` | S=`She`；V=`is`；C=`my English teacher` | 她是我的英语老师。 |
| `We are ready for the lesson.` | S=`We`；V=`are`；C=`ready`；A=`for the lesson` | 我们准备好上课了。 |
| `They are at the bus stop.` | S=`They`；V=`are`；C=`at the bus stop` | 他们在公交车站。 |
| `It is cold outside.` | `it` 指天气/环境；`is cold` 是状态 | 外面很冷。中文通常不译“它”。 |
| `These books are hers.` | S=`These books`；V=`are`；C=`hers` | 这些书是她的。`hers` 后不能再接 books。 |

### 详细对比

```text
She is my teacher.     she：主格，做主语；my：后面带 teacher
I study with her.      her：在介词 with 后，用宾格
This book is hers.     hers：独立使用，等于 her book
She wrote it herself.  herself：强调她亲自写
```

**替换模板**：`人 + be + 身份/形容词/位置`。用 `I/you/he/they` 分别替换，注意 am/is/are 跟着变化。

## 2. there be 与 have：都有“有”，视角不同

| 例句 | 语法焦点 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `There is a café near my office.` | 引入一个此前未提到的 café | 我的办公室附近有一家咖啡馆。 |
| `There are two messages for you.` | 真正对象是复数 messages，所以用 are | 有两条给你的消息。 |
| `There isn't enough time.` | 不可数 time 与 is 搭配 | 时间不够。 |
| `Is there a restroom nearby?` | be 提前形成疑问 | 附近有洗手间吗？ |
| `I have a meeting at ten.` | I 拥有/安排有一场会议 | 我十点有个会。 |
| `The room has two windows.` | room 具有两个窗户 | 这个房间有两扇窗。 |

`There is a problem.` 是“出现/存在一个问题”；`We have a problem.` 是“我们面临一个问题”。两句描述的事实接近，但信息视角不同。

**高频词块**：`There is no ...` 没有……；`There are several ...` 有几个……；`have time to do` 有时间做……；`have a problem with` 在……方面有问题。

## 3. 一般现在时：习惯、事实和稳定行为

| 例句 | 为什么用一般现在时 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `I walk to work every day.` | 我的日常习惯；I 后用原形 walk | 我每天步行上班。 |
| `She studies English after dinner.` | 她的习惯；she 后 study 变 studies | 她晚饭后学英语。 |
| `My brother works from home.` | 单数 brother，work 加 s | 我哥哥在家办公。 |
| `The shop opens at nine.` | 固定营业时间 | 这家店九点开门。 |
| `Water boils at 100°C.` | 一般事实 | 水在 100 摄氏度时沸腾。 |
| `This button opens the menu.` | 产品的稳定功能 | 这个按钮用于打开菜单。 |

### 三单变化不是“复数”

```text
I work.       We work.       They work.
He works.     Maya works.    The app works.
```

三单 `-s` 标在动词上，说明一般现在时的主语是 he/she/it 或一个单数对象。它和名词复数的 `-s` 作用不同。

**替换练习**：把 `She studies English.` 的主语依次改成 `I / Tom / They`，得到 `I study / Tom studies / They study`。

## 4. do/does：一般现在时的否定和疑问

| 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 | 关键点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `I need help.` | `I don't need help.` | `Do you need help?` | do 承担否定/疑问，need 用原形 |
| `She likes coffee.` | `She doesn't like coffee.` | `Does she like coffee?` | 三单 -s 移到 does 上 |
| `They work here.` | `They don't work here.` | `Do they work here?` | they 使用 do |
| `The train stops here.` | `The train doesn't stop here.` | `Does the train stop here?` | 不说 `doesn't stops` |
| `He knows the answer.` | `He doesn't know the answer.` | `Does he know the answer?` | knows → does + know |

### 疑问词放在最前面

| 问题 | 拆解 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `Where do you live?` | where + do + you + live | 你住在哪里？ |
| `What does this word mean?` | what + does + this word + mean | 这个词是什么意思？ |
| `Why does she need it?` | why + does + she + need + it | 她为什么需要它？ |
| `When do we start?` | when + do + we + start | 我们什么时候开始？ |

**回答不要只背 Yes/No**：`Does she work here?` → `Yes, she does. She works on the second floor.`

## 5. be + doing：此刻或当前阶段正在发生

| 例句 | 完整动词组 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `I am waiting for the bus.` | `am waiting` | 我正在等公交车。 |
| `She is talking to a customer.` | `is talking` | 她正在和一位顾客交谈。 |
| `We are learning how to ask questions.` | `are learning` | 我们正在学习怎样提问。 |
| `They are staying with friends this week.` | `are staying`，当前这周的临时安排 | 他们这周暂住在朋友家。 |
| `The weather is getting colder.` | `is getting`，变化正在发展 | 天气正变得越来越冷。 |
| `The program is running in the background.` | `is running` | 程序正在后台运行。 |

对比：`I work from home.` 表示通常如此；`I am working from home this week.` 表示这周的临时状态。

**否定和疑问**：`She isn't sleeping.`；`Is she sleeping?`。be 已经承担语法，不再使用 do。

## 6. 一般过去时：已经结束的过去事件

| 例句 | 过去形式 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `I called my mother last night.` | call → called | 我昨晚给母亲打了电话。 |
| `She went to the bank this morning.` | go → went | 她今天早上去了银行。 |
| `We had lunch near the station.` | have → had | 我们在车站附近吃了午饭。 |
| `He wrote a short email.` | write → wrote | 他写了一封简短邮件。 |
| `The meeting started late.` | start → started | 会议开始得很晚。 |
| `The file opened without an error.` | open → opened | 文件顺利打开了，没有报错。 |

### did 出现后，实义动词恢复原形

```text
She went home.
She didn't go home.       不是 didn't went
Did she go home?          不是 Did she went
Where did she go?
```

**高频过去时间**：`yesterday`、`last night/week/year`、`two days ago`、`this morning`、`when I was ...`。

## 7. 将来、能力、建议和礼貌

| 例句 | 情态/结构的作用 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `I will call you tomorrow.` | will：将来/意愿 | 我明天给你打电话。 |
| `We are going to visit Suzhou.` | be going to：已有打算 | 我们打算去苏州。 |
| `Can you help me?` | can：请求；语气直接但正常 | 你能帮我吗？ |
| `Could you say that again?` | could：更礼貌的请求 | 你能再说一遍吗？ |
| `You should take a break.` | should：建议 | 你应该休息一下。 |
| `You must show your ID.` | must：强制要求 | 你必须出示身份证件。 |
| `It may rain this afternoon.` | may：可能 | 今天下午可能下雨。 |
| `I'd like a window seat.` | would like：礼貌表达想要 | 我想要一个靠窗座位。 |

情态动词没有三单变化，后接原形：`She can speak English.`，不是 `can speaks`。

**力度对比**：`You must leave.` 必须离开；`You should leave.` 建议离开；`You may leave.` 可以离开。

## 8. 祈使句、请求和建议

| 例句 | 暗含主语/结构 | 使用场景与翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `Please sit down.` | `(You) sit` | 请坐。日常礼貌指令。 |
| `Turn left at the next corner.` | `(You) turn` | 在下一个路口左转。 |
| `Don't worry.` | `Do not + 原形` | 别担心。 |
| `Let's take a short break.` | `Let us + 原形` | 我们短暂休息一下吧。 |
| `Could you open the window?` | 情态疑问，更像请求 | 你能打开窗户吗？ |
| `Would you like some tea?` | 礼貌邀请/提供 | 你想喝点茶吗？ |
| `Save your work before you close the app.` | 指令 + before 条件/时间 | 关闭应用前请保存工作。 |

`Open the door.` 语法正确，但对陌生人可能太直接；日常请求可用 `Could you open the door, please?`。操作说明则通常直接用祈使句，不需要反复添加 please。

## 9. 系动词 + 形容词：描述而不是动作

| 例句 | 系动词 | 自然理解 |
|---|---|---|
| `You look tired.` | look | 你看起来很累。tired 描述 you。 |
| `That sounds like a good plan.` | sound | 那听起来是个好计划。 |
| `The soup smells wonderful.` | smell | 这汤闻起来很香。 |
| `This coffee tastes too strong.` | taste | 这杯咖啡味道太浓。 |
| `I feel nervous before meetings.` | feel | 我开会前会感到紧张。 |
| `The room became quiet.` | become | 房间安静了下来。 |
| `Please stay safe.` | stay | 请注意安全。 |
| `The reason remains unclear.` | remain | 原因仍不清楚。 |

形容词通常不加 `-ly`：说 `The food tastes good.`，不说 `tastes well` 来表示味道好。`well` 可作形容词表示身体健康：`I don't feel well.` 我感觉不舒服。

## 10. 高频介词：不要只背一个中文意思

| 介词 | 关系 | 例句与翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| at | 时间点/具体点位 | `Meet me at the station at six.` 六点在车站和我见面。 |
| on | 日期/表面 | `The class is on Monday.` 课程在周一。`The phone is on the table.` 手机在桌上。 |
| in | 较大时间/内部 | `I was born in 1990.` 我出生于 1990 年。`She is in the room.` 她在房间里。 |
| to | 方向/接收者/不定式 | `Send the message to me.` 把消息发给我。 |
| from | 来源/起点 | `This train goes from Shanghai to Nanjing.` 这趟车从上海开往南京。 |
| for | 受益者/用途/时长 | `This gift is for you.` 这份礼物是给你的。`I waited for an hour.` 我等了一小时。 |
| with | 一起/使用工具/带有 | `I went with Anna.` 我和 Anna 一起去了。`Cut it with a knife.` 用刀切。 |
| by | 执行者/方法/截止 | `The book was written by her.` 这本书由她所写。`Finish it by Friday.` 周五前完成。 |
| about | 关于/大约 | `We talked about the plan.` 我们讨论了计划。 |
| of | 所属、组成、内容 | `a cup of tea` 一杯茶；`the end of the day` 一天结束时 |

介词要和词一起记：`listen to`、`wait for`、`talk about`、`work with`、`depend on`、`be afraid of`。

## 11. 连接词：把两个简单句组织成逻辑

| 例句 | 逻辑 | 翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `I was tired, but I finished the work.` | but：事实转折 | 我很累，但还是完成了工作。 |
| `I stayed home because it was raining.` | because + 原因句 | 因为下雨，我待在家里。 |
| `It was raining, so I stayed home.` | so + 结果句 | 当时在下雨，所以我待在家里。 |
| `If you need help, call me.` | if：条件可能发生 | 如果需要帮助，就给我打电话。 |
| `When you arrive, send me a message.` | when：预期会发生的时间点 | 到了以后给我发消息。 |
| `Wait here until the bus comes.` | until：持续到某时 | 在这里等到公交车来。 |
| `Although the room was small, it was comfortable.` | although：承认事实后转折 | 房间虽小，却很舒适。 |
| `Check the address before you send the package.` | before：动作先后 | 寄包裹前检查地址。 |

### 同一事实的不同组织

```text
I missed the bus because I left home late.  我错过公交，因为出门晚了。
I left home late, so I missed the bus.      我出门晚了，所以错过公交。
```

两句事实相同，但 because 后面放原因，so 后面放结果。

## 12. to do、doing、done 和被动

| 结构 | 例句 | 作用和翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| `want to do` | `I want to improve my English.` | want 的内容：我想提高英语。 |
| `need to do` | `We need to leave now.` | 需要做：我们现在得走。 |
| `to do` 表目的 | `I went out to buy some milk.` | 出门的目的：我出去买牛奶。 |
| 介词 + doing | `Thank you for helping me.` | for 后用 doing：谢谢你帮助我。 |
| `be + doing` | `The children are playing outside.` | 正在进行：孩子们正在外面玩。 |
| doing 修饰名词 | `the woman waiting outside` | 正在外面等候的女士 |
| `have + done` | `I have finished my homework.` | 已经完成且与现在有关：我已经做完作业。 |
| `be + done` | `The road is closed.` | 被动/结果状态：道路已封闭。 |
| done 修饰名词 | `the message sent yesterday` | 昨天发出的消息 |
| `be used to do` | `This card is used to open the door.` | 卡片被用于开门；used 是过去分词 |

### 过去式与过去分词对比

```text
She wrote the message yesterday.      wrote 独立作谓语：过去写了
She has written the message.          has + written：已经写好
The message was written by her.       was + written：由她写
The message written by her was clear. written by her：修饰 message
```

过去分词本身不等于“过去”。它需要和 `have`、`be` 或名词位置一起判断功能。

## 精读短文 1：A Normal Morning（日常，A1-A2）

### English

I usually get up at seven. My wife gets up earlier because she starts work at eight. While she makes breakfast, I open the windows and feed our cat. We have coffee together, but we do not have much time to talk. At seven thirty, she leaves home and takes the bus. I work from home, so I start my day at the desk in our bedroom.

### 中文核验

我通常七点起床。我的妻子起得更早，因为她八点开始工作。她做早餐时，我打开窗户并喂猫。我们一起喝咖啡，但没有太多时间聊天。七点半，她离开家去乘公交车。我在家办公，所以会在卧室里的书桌前开始一天的工作。

### 结构解析

- `usually` 表频率，因此全文主要使用一般现在时。
- `My wife` 是单数，动词用 `gets / starts / makes / leaves / takes`。
- `because she starts work at eight` 给出起得早的原因。
- `While she makes breakfast` 表示两个动作同时发生。
- `do not have` 中 do 承担否定，have 保持原形。
- `work from home` 是“在家办公”的固定表达；`at the desk` 是工作位置。

**核心词块**：`get up`、`start work`、`make breakfast`、`have time to talk`、`work from home`

## 精读短文 2：Learning One Small Thing（学习，A2）

### English

Today I am learning how to ask questions in English. At first, the word order seems difficult. I can understand “You need help,” but I sometimes forget to say “Do you need help?” My teacher tells me to look for the helping verb first. I read each example aloud and then change one part. After ten minutes, the pattern becomes clearer. I still make mistakes, but I know how to correct them.

### 中文核验

今天我正在学习如何用英语提问。起初，词序看起来很难。我能理解“You need help”，但有时会忘记说成疑问句“Do you need help?”。老师让我先寻找助动词。我大声朗读每个例句，然后替换其中一个部分。十分钟后，这个句型变得更清楚了。我仍然会犯错，但已经知道怎样改正。

### 结构解析

- `am learning` 表示今天当前阶段正在学习。
- `how to ask questions` 是“怎样提问”，整体作 learning 的内容。
- `seems difficult / becomes clearer` 都是“系动词 + 形容词”。
- `can understand` 中 can 后接原形。
- `forget to say` 表示忘记去做；`tell + 人 + to do` 表示告诉某人做某事。
- `make mistakes / correct them` 中 them 指前面的 mistakes。

**核心词块**：`learn how to`、`at first`、`forget to`、`look for`、`read aloud`、`make a mistake`

## 精读短文 3：A Clear Work Message（工作，A2）

### English

Leo received a task yesterday, but some information was missing. He did not wait for the problem to solve itself. He wrote to his manager: “I have started the report, and the first section is ready. However, I cannot finish the cost table because I do not have the latest prices. Could you send them to me before three? If I receive them today, I will finish the report by five.”

### 中文核验

Leo 昨天接到一项任务，但缺少一些信息。他没有等着问题自行解决，而是给经理写道：“我已经开始编写报告，第一部分也准备好了。不过，我无法完成成本表，因为没有最新价格。你能在三点前把价格发给我吗？如果今天收到，我会在五点前完成报告。”

### 结构解析

- `received / was` 描述昨天发生或存在的过去情况。
- `did not wait` 中 did 已表示过去，wait 恢复原形。
- `itself` 指 problem 自己；`solve itself` 是“自行解决”。
- `have started` 强调工作已经开始，结果与现在相关。
- `cannot finish ... because ...` 是结果在前、原因在后。
- `Could you send them ...?` 是礼貌请求，them 指 latest prices。
- `If + 一般现在时, will + 原形` 表示真实可能的未来条件。
- 两个 `by` 都表示“不晚于某个时间点”。

**核心词块**：`receive a task`、`wait for`、`be ready`、`the latest`、`send ... to ...`、`by five`

## 精读短文 4：Changing Trains（出行，A2-B1）

### English

Our train was delayed, so we arrived at the next station twenty minutes late. The direct bus had already left. There was another bus at four, but it stopped at every small town and took two hours. We asked a station worker for advice. She showed us a faster route and said, “Take the local train to West Park, then change to bus 12. Keep your ticket because it is checked when you leave the station.”

### 中文核验

我们的火车晚点了，所以到达下一站时迟了二十分钟。直达公交已经开走。四点还有另一班公交，但它每个小镇都停，全程需要两个小时。我们向车站工作人员寻求建议。她给我们看了一条更快的路线，并说：“乘普通列车到 West Park，然后换乘 12 路公交。请保留车票，因为出站时会查票。”

### 结构解析

- `was delayed` 是 be + 过去分词，表示火车被延误/晚点。
- `so` 后面是晚点造成的结果。
- `had already left` 表示在我们到站之前，公交已经离开；入门阶段整体理解为“早已开走”即可。
- `There was another bus` 引入另一班公交。
- `ask + 人 + for + 事物`：向某人请求某物；这里是寻求建议。
- 引号中的 `Take / change / Keep` 是三个祈使句。
- `it is checked` 中 it 指 ticket；票是被检查的对象。
- `when you leave` 给出查票发生的时间。

**核心词块**：`be delayed`、`arrive at`、`ask ... for advice`、`show someone a route`、`change to`、`keep your ticket`

## 综合复习方法

对每篇短文完成四次输出：

1. 圈出所有完整动词组，并说明为什么是这个形式。
2. 找出所有代词，画箭头指向它们代表的人或事物。
3. 遮住译文，用中文准确复述，不遗漏否定、时间和条件。
4. 用自己的生活或工作内容写一个结构相同的 5-8 句短段落。

如果第四步写不出来，先复用原文骨架，只替换人物、时间、地点和名词；能正确替换比强行写复杂句更重要。
